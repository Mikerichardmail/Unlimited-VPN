Frequently Asked Questions

How does the 3-Day Free Trial work?
When you subscribe to any plan via the Unlimited VPN Android app on Google Play, Google Play Billing gives you full access for 3 days without charging your account. You can cancel anytime in Google Play Store settings before the trial ends.
Does Unlimited VPN keep any browsing logs?

No. We operate under a strict privacy policy. We do not store, monitor, or track your DNS queries or web traffic.

Why is WireGuard® better for Android gaming and streaming?
WireGuard® uses high-performance modern crypto primitives (ChaCha20-Poly1305) that process data directly inside the Linux kernel on Android, resulting in up to 3x higher throughput and significantly lower ping than OpenVPN.
