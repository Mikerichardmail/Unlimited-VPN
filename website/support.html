Support & Setup Guide

Get Unlimited VPN running on your Android device in less than 2 minutes.

Android Quick Installation Guide

Step 1: Install from Google Play

Download Unlimited VPN directly from the official Google Play Store listing (com.bestfreevpnproxy.app).

Step 2: Grant Android VpnService Permission

Upon launching the app, tap Connect. Android will display a standard system prompt: "Unlimited VPN wants to set up a VPN connection". Tap OK.

Step 3: Select Fast Location

Choose your preferred server (e.g. India - Mumbai for lowest local ping) and enjoy 3 days of unrestricted free access.

Common Troubleshooting

Connection Times Out: Ensure mobile data or Wi-Fi is active. Switch to another server location in the server picker.

Google Play Billing Error: Ensure your Google account is signed into Play Store with an active payment method registered for trial validation.
