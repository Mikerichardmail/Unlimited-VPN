Privacy Policy & No-Logs Guarantee

Last updated: July 31, 2026. Your digital privacy and anonymity are our absolute priority.

1. Strict 100% No-Logs Guarantee

Unlimited VPN operates under a strict, non-negotiable No-Logs Policy. We do NOT record, log, store, or monitor any of the following data under any circumstances:

  • No Traffic Logs: We do not log destination IP addresses, websites visited, DNS requests, or application data transferred through our WireGuard® servers.
  • No Connection Timestamps: We do not log connection start times, disconnection times, or session duration.
  • No IP Address Logs: We do not log your real public IP address or the VPN IP address assigned to your device.
  • No Bandwidth Tracking per User: We do not keep logs of individual data volume consumption.

2. Data Collected & Processed

To maintain active subscription access and deliver backend connection endpoints, we only process the minimal essential data:

  • Account & Subscription Data: Anonymous purchase token generated via Google Play Billing API to verify active trial/subscription status. No credit card details are ever stored on Unlimited VPN servers.
  • Anonymous Crash Reports: Optional, aggregated crash stack trace telemetry (without device identifiers or IP addresses) to fix Android app crashes.

3. WireGuard® Cryptography & Security

Unlimited VPN utilizes the modern WireGuard® VPN protocol with high-grade ChaCha20-Poly1305 encryption. Ephemeral key exchanges occur dynamically via the Noise protocol. Key pairs are stored strictly in volatile RAM memory on VPN nodes and wiped upon container restart.

4. Third-Party Services & Google Play Billing

Payments and subscription management are handled securely through Google Play Store (Google LLC). Please review Google Privacy Policy regarding payment credential processing. Unlimited VPN backend servers interact with Google Play Real-Time Developer Notifications (RTDN) webhooks purely to validate subscription active state tokens.

5. Children's Privacy

Unlimited VPN does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal information, please contact us immediately for deletion.
